READ THE MODEL

A simple equation. Clear limits.

Score = 37.2329 + 6.0713 × hours

Linear Regression learns its slope and intercept from the training set. The intercept represents zero hours, outside the observed data range.

Only study hours is a predictor. A larger, representative sample and features such as prior grades, attendance, and sleep would be needed for a more realistic future model.
